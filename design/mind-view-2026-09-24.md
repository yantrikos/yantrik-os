# Mind View — the spike, and the first slice (#239)

A mind opening an app put that window on the person's desktop, over their work and over the
Lens's approval card (#205, #218, #230). Mind View gives the mind a desktop of its own inside one
window: a nested labwc, with the apps a mind opens started on its display.

## What the spike established

Run in a cloud container (Ubuntu 24.04, labwc 0.7.1 on wlroots 0.17, pixman renderer, 4 cores),
with a headless labwc standing in for the person's session. Not yet on VM 520, whose labwc is
0.8.3.

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| Does labwc run nested? | Yes: `WLR_BACKENDS=wayland labwc -C <dir>` under the person's labwc is one window, titled `wlroots - WL-1`, app_id `wlroots`. |
| Do apps land inside it? | Yes. A client started with `WAYLAND_DISPLAY=<nested>` drew inside that window and not on the outer desktop; a client on the outer display stayed outside. |
| X apps? | The nested labwc runs its own Xwayland. An `xeyes` on its `DISPLAY` reported the nested output's size (1278×695, not the outer 1280×720) and drew inside Mind View. |
| How does the shell learn the display names? | labwc chooses the first free `wayland-N` itself. Its `-s` startup command runs with the nested `WAYLAND_DISPLAY` and `DISPLAY` in its environment, so a two-line script writes both to a file the shell reads. |
| Can it be resized? | Setting the nested output's mode (`wlr-randr --output WL-1 --custom-mode 800x500`) resized the window on the outer desktop to match. Dragging the window's edge works the other way round, like any window. |
| CPU | Idle: 0.0% for both compositors over 10 s. With a terminal inside Mind View redrawing without pause: nested labwc 3.5%, outer 2.4%, the terminal itself 12%. A second compositor is a second rasteriser, and on pixman it is cheap. |

**One problem, specific to wlroots 0.17 as far as the spike could tell.** Started soon after the
outer compositor, the nested labwc often stalled in `backend/wayland` output creation: the outer
had created the xdg_toplevel, but the nested one sat in `epoll_wait` and never ran its startup
command. Interrupting it (`strace -p`) let it continue, which points at a missed wakeup rather
than a missing configure. SIGHUP did not help. It has to be checked on 0.8.3 before anything
else. The first slice does not depend on it being fixed: see "Failing safe" below.

## The first slice

- **Routing** (`crates/yantrik-ui/src/mind_view.rs`, `wire/dock.rs::spawn_launch`). Every shell
  launch ends in `spawn_launch`, on the UI thread, while the caller that asked for it is still in
  scope. `route_now` sorts that caller:
  - a click (no caller) is the person;
  - an agent token, believed or not, is a mind;
  - the shell's own pid is the built-in companion, a mind;
  - a process descended from an attached harness is that mind;
  - anything else (`yos` typed in the Terminal, labwc's Ctrl+Alt+T) is the person.

  A mind's launch goes to Mind View, started on a worker thread if it is not up yet.
- **Not raised over the person's work.** An app already open on the person's desktop is not
  opened a second time. Our apps are single-instance, so the mind drives that window through its
  surface, and the handover no longer raises it when a mind asked.
- **The window list.** An app drawing in Mind View is not listed as one of the person's windows.
  The nested compositor's own window is listed as `mind-view`, subtitled "Mind View". Its title
  is kept as wlroots wrote it, because the title is what `wlrctl` finds it by.
- **`describe shell` → `mind_view`**: whether minds' apps go there, whether it is running and on
  which display, which apps are in it, and why not if it could not start.
- **The choice.** The last row of the mode menu reads "Apps it opens go · in Mind View / on my
  desktop". It is on by default, as the issue asked. It is a UI callback like the modes, and
  nothing on the socket can set it.
- **Its compositor's configuration**: `config/labwc-mind/rc.xml`, shipped to
  `/opt/yantrik/share/labwc-mind`.

### Failing safe

Mind View fails safe in three cases:
- labwc is not installed;
- its configuration is missing;
- it does not report its display within 5 s.

In each case the app opens on the person's desktop, exactly as before. The reason is logged and
shown under `mind_view.unavailable`, and Mind View is not tried again until the shell restarts.
The wait is paid on a worker thread, never on the UI thread.

## The second slice: the window itself

The first slice opened Mind View wherever labwc puts a new window: at wlroots' default 1280×720,
which on the VM's 1280×800 is the whole screen. It also took the keyboard, and the taskbar
called it "wlroots - WL-1".

- **Where it opens.** A window rule in the person's `config/labwc/rc.xml` matches
  `identifier="wlroots" title="wlroots - *"` and runs three actions: `ResizeTo 760×500`, then
  `MoveToEdge right`, then `MoveToEdge down`, both with `snapWindows="no"`. It opens in the
  bottom-right corner and stops at the taskbar's 40px `<margin>`. 760×500 is the smallest display
  that holds one of our apps: they ask for at least 640×400, plus the title bar and border the
  nested labwc draws.
- **What is inside it.** Mind View's own `config/labwc-mind/rc.xml` maximizes every window.
  It is a view onto the mind's work, not a desktop to arrange: an app opening at its preferred
  1100×800 inside 760×500 would be mostly off the edge.
- **The keyboard.** labwc focuses and raises a new window before any window rule runs
  (`view_impl_map` in labwc 0.8.3), so no rule can stop Mind View taking the keyboard as it
  opens. The shell hands it back instead: `mind_view::hand_back_focus` notes the person's front
  window before starting Mind View, watches for Mind View to take focus, and focuses their window
  again. When the desktop itself was in front it leaves Mind View where it is, because raising
  the fullscreen shell would hide Mind View the moment it appeared.
- **Its name.** The taskbar and the window switcher say "Mind View". The title stays wlroots' own,
  because that title is what they hand `wlrctl` to find the window.

**Verified on labwc 0.7.1**, the version this container has:
- with `config/labwc/rc.xml` itself on a 1280×800 headless output, a window declaring Mind View's
  app_id and title opened at x 518–1280, y 236–760, with 760×500 of content above the taskbar
  margin;
- `wlrctl toplevel focus` gave the keyboard back to the person's window;
- the maximize rule filled a 760×500 output.

**Not verified on 0.8.3.** No source for wlroots 0.18 is reachable from this container (freedesktop,
the Debian mirrors and GitHub mirrors were all blocked or lacked it). labwc 0.8.3's own source
was read instead: `labwc-actions(5)` has `ResizeTo`, `MoveToEdge` with `snapWindows` and
`Maximize`, and `window_rules_apply(ON_FIRST_MAP)` still runs after the focus.

## Not in this slice

- **A better title bar** than `wlroots - WL-1` on the window's own decoration. wlroots sets the
  title itself, and labwc exposes no way to change it; the taskbar is renamed, the title bar is
  not.
- **"Bring to my desktop"**: reopening an app with the same document on the person's display.
- **The other direction of the single-instance handover.** If a mind opened Notes in Mind View
  and the person then clicks Notes, the second copy hands over to the Mind View window, and the
  person sees nothing on their own desktop. "Bring to my desktop" is what answers it.
- **Launches that bypass `spawn_launch`**:
  - the Lens's desktop-entry results (`wire/lens.rs`, `wire/chat.rs`);
  - the companion's `browser` tools, which hardcode `wayland-0`;
  - `apply_workspace_template`;
  - GUI programs started from `agent_run`, which get no display at all today.
- **Screenshots, perception's screen reads and `wlrctl` window actions** still act on the
  person's display only.
- **Links from the Agents Overview (#226) and task progress (#234).**
- **A release-check** that opens an app as a mind and asserts it landed in Mind View.
