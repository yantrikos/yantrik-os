# UI punch list, build 1461 (12 Oct 2026)

From a full sweep of VM 520 on build v0.1.0-1461-gb056da0f (main plus the approved PRs): every app's main window, every Settings section and every shell screen, 44 screens. Each was reviewed against design/experience-final-2026-10-10.md and design/ux-psychology-2026-10-11.md by Qwen3.8 27B (F:/yantrik/coder/ui_review.py); findings are grouped by punchlist.py. Every text finding quotes what was on screen. A model's review is evidence, not a verdict: check each item against the screen before changing code.

115 distinct problems on 44 screens (33 high). Repeated problems are listed once under Everywhere.
