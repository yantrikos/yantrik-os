# The Bench: desktop synthesis spec

Built on team FABLE's Bench, folding in team Y's threads, ask-the-bench, memory lines and Resume, and team Codex's "bring a Mind's branch to your desk". Six clean frames in `frame-1..6.png`, generated by `build.py` into `concept.html`.

## The six frames
1. **Everyday Bench.** Shortcuts and files where Pranab put them, one label treatment, real thumbnails, origin marks (◆ Verified, ◇ Reported), a Hermes "writing" placeholder, and the bottom strip: day rail + three threads with one "where you left it" line each.
2. **Lineage.** Hold Super on a file; threads draw to the Lens request, the Mind's Verified write (call filmstrip, opens Mind View), the source file and the project.
3. **Ask the bench.** Typing in the ask bar spotlights the matches in place and dims the rest; a card says how they matched, "No Mind was asked", and offers Gather / Move… / Leave them, with Undo.
4. **Arrange views.** Free / By origin / By project / By time regroup items as a view only; Keep writes positions; File away moves and asks first.
5. **Scrub + Resume.** The Bench at Tue 16:40: later arrivals as ghosts, moved files tagged, a card with what was open, what you asked Hermes (◆ OS record), the decision you noted (◇ remembered), and **Resume Starfall**, "Opens apps only, where they were. Minds wait for you."
6. **Bring it to your bench.** Hermes finished a working copy in its own desk; an amber proposal tied to the original shows both previews, the ◆ Verified write receipt, a ◇ Reported claim beside a red failed test, and Replace (keeps the 14:21 version for Undo) / Keep both / Not now.

### Why threads live in the bottom strip, not an edge panel
"Where you left it" is a statement about time, and the day rail is already the Bench's time control, so the two belong in one 44px band. A right-edge panel was round 2's mistake: it ate a third of the bench and turned files into a sidebar's neighbours. In the strip, threads cost no bench area, stay legible (title, age, one line), and act as controls: click a thread to ring its files on the bench (a view), long-press to scrub to its last moment. The strip can collapse to the rail alone from the right-click menu.

### Palette and truth rules applied
- Amber only on frame 6 (needs the person) and on "needed you" rail ticks. Teal only for Minds. Violet unused (nobody is driving in these frames).
- ◆ never appears without an OS receipt; ◇ appears for claims and for memory; unmediated files carry no mark and say "origin unknown" in lineage.
- Views and scrub change nothing on disk. Keep writes positions. Only Move, File away and Replace write, all under approval, all with Undo, archive never delete.
- No slogans; every string is a label, a fact or a control. NEW marks are kept out of the product frames and live in this spec.

## Renderer notes
Software renderer: wallpaper and thumbnails are bitmaps; the dim is a flat alpha fill; threads are a handful of 1.5px curves; regions and the proposal card are flat panels with 1px borders; views, scrub and lineage are full redraws on interaction only; no idle animation, no timers (the rail ticks are redrawn when a journal entry is appended).

## Build order
**Phase 1: the bench itself.** Create and list `~/Desktop`; icons and app shortcuts; positions store with free placement, snapping and drag and drop; thumbnail cache (png, pdf, blend); one label treatment; desktop and item menus (New folder, New file, Rename, Open with, Reveal, Archive); Super+D keeps working; `describe shell` gains `desktop.items` and `act` gains `place` (sensitive) and `archive` (sensitive). Ship when a person can use it like any other desktop.

**Phase 2: provenance.** Provenance index written by the control surface on mediated writes and claims; origin marks; the "writing" placeholder; Lens request log; lineage (hold Super). Unmediated = origin unknown.

**Phase 3: views and ask.** `bench_views.rs` (Free / By origin / By project / By time), persisted project links, stacks (Tray and Gather), Keep, File away under approval, ask-the-bench spotlight with the local word index and window-title history.

**Phase 4: the journal.** Layout journal and window-set snapshots; the day rail with ticks; scrub with ghosts and gaps; threads in the bottom strip with "where you left it"; the memory hook for ◇ decisions.

**Phase 5: hand-offs.** Resume (session restore, apps only); Mind handoff records with hash-bound Replace / Keep both / Not now; the amber proposal on the bench.

Each phase is independently useful and each later phase only adds truth sources; none of them moves a file without the person.
