# Capture: screenshots and screen recording

15 Oct 2026. Pranab: "we need tools like screenshot, screen recorder etc."

## What exists

- **Print** (labwc keybind, `build-debian-iso.sh`) saves a full-screen PNG to
  `~/Pictures/Screenshots/<date>_<time>.png` with `grim`. It gives no feedback, offers no region, and
  copies nothing to the clipboard.
- **The image** has `grim`, `slurp`, `wl-clipboard`, `xdg-desktop-portal-wlr` (screencast over
  PipeWire) and `mpv`. It has no recorder.
- **For minds**, `control_screen.rs` has `read_screen` (the person's desktop as OCR text:
  `sensitive`, cannot be undone, one card per read in every mode but full bypass) and
  `read_mind_view` (Mind View, the minds' own display: `safe`). There is no picture capture for
  anyone.
- **labwc 0.8 tells no client where a window is**, so per-window capture needs the person to draw the
  window's rectangle. Version 1 offers region and full screen; "window" comes when the compositor
  can tell us.

## For the person

**The capture bar.** Print opens it; Esc closes it. It is a small bar at the bottom centre, drawn by
the shell, opaque, in the shell's own components:

- **Screenshot | Record**
- **Region | Screen**
- **Timer:** off, 3 s or 10 s
- **Pointer:** shown or hidden
- **Audio** (Record only): none, system, or microphone
- **Capture** or **Start**

Region uses `slurp`: the person drags a rectangle, and Esc cancels.

**Shortcuts:**
- **Shift+Print:** region screenshot, straight to the selection.
- **Ctrl+Print:** full-screen screenshot to the clipboard only.
- **Super+Shift+S:** region screenshot (familiar from other desktops).
- **Super+Shift+R:** start or stop a recording (opens the bar in Record mode when none is running).
- **Plain Print** opens the bar. Today it saves silently; the bar is the change people will see.

**After a screenshot.** The PNG is saved to `~/Pictures/Screenshots/Screenshot <YYYY-MM-DD> at
<HH.MM.SS>.png` and copied to the clipboard (`wl-copy --type image/png`). A notification with a
thumbnail offers Open, Show in Files and Delete. The bar and the notification are never in the
picture: they hide before the capture, and the capture waits for the frame after they are gone.

**Recording.** `wf-recorder` (Debian 13: 0.5.0, MIT) to `~/Videos/Recordings/Recording <date> at
<time>.mp4`.
- **Encoding.** H.264 through VA-API when `/dev/dri/renderD128` encodes. Otherwise `libx264`, preset
  `veryfast`, CRF 23.
- **Rate and audio.** 30 fps by default (the bar may offer 60 where the hardware encodes). Audio
  from PipeWire when chosen.
- **While it runs:** a red dot with the elapsed time sits in the status bar, and clicking it stops
  the recording. Super+Shift+R also stops it.
- **Stopping by itself:** at lock or logout, cleanly, so the file is never truncated; and when the
  disk has under 1 GB free, saying so.
- **The finished file:** a notification offers Open (mpv), Show in Files and Delete.

**Where the recorder runs.** In a transient user service of its own (`systemd-run --user --unit
yantrik-capture-rec-<n>`), so it can be stopped and accounted for. It runs at normal weight; it is
the person's work, not an agent's (see `design/resource-management-2026-10-15.md`). Its CPU and
memory show in System Monitor under the name "Screen recording".

## For minds (the control surface)

Published on the shell's surface as a `capture` group, beside `read_screen`. Its classification is
the same as reading the screen, because a picture holds even more than its text.

| Action | Grade |
|---|---|
| `screenshot target=mind_view` | `safe`. A mind photographing its own display, to check its own work. |
| `screenshot target=screen` and `target=region` (the person's desktop) | `sensitive`. Its purpose says it cannot be undone, word for word as `read_screen` does, so the gate asks every time and no session rule covers it. |

The path of the PNG is returned. It is saved where the person would find it, and the person is
notified that a mind took it, naming the mind.

- **Recording by a mind:** Mind View only (`record_start target=mind_view`, `record_stop`), graded
  `sensitive`. Recording the person's desktop is never offered to a mind, at any grade. The
  red-dot indicator shows for every recording and cannot be hidden by any caller.
- **`describe shell`** gains `capture`: whether a recording runs, since when, what it records, who
  started it, and where it will be saved.
- **Locked desktop:** nothing is captured, by anyone. The state rule already refuses every act
  while locked, and this holds it for the keybinds too.
- **The private-read taint:** a picture of the person's desktop taints the conversation exactly as
  `read_screen` does (`yos-mcp` `PRIVATE_READS` / `SCREEN_READS`).
- **Saved captures:** a screenshot or recording on disk is the same picture, so it is graded and
  tainted the same wherever a mind could reach it (security review of #828). `~/Pictures/Screenshots`
  and `~/Videos/Recordings` are on the shared protected list (`home_paths::PROTECTED`): Files, the
  apps' `open`, the companion's file tools and the walks and archives they make all leave them out.
  A mind's one way to one is `open_capture`: `sensitive`, its purpose saying it cannot be undone as
  `read_screen`'s does, tainted as `read_screen` in both the companion and `yos-mcp`.

## Packaging

- Add `wf-recorder` to the ISO's package list. Its libav dependencies are already there for mpv.
- Add it to the update bundle's binary checks (`app-bins.sh` or wherever release-check lists
  required binaries) and to `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`.
- Change the labwc keybinds in `build-debian-iso.sh`, so Print and the others go to the shell (a
  `yos act shell capture_*` call, or a small `yantrik-capture` helper the shell owns), and the
  installed system's copy of rc.xml with them.

## Tests

- **Unit tests:** file names; the choice of encoder (`renderD128` present and encoding, versus
  missing); the disk-space guard; the stop on lock; the grades and purposes of every action,
  including that no target lets a mind record the person's desktop.
- **The preview harness** (`tests/ui-preview`): the bar in each mode, both themes, at 1280×800 and at
  a narrow width.
- **On a test VM:**
  - a region screenshot lands, is on the clipboard and never shows the bar;
  - a 30 s recording plays in mpv and is at least 28 s long;
  - the red dot stops it;
  - lock stops it cleanly;
  - a mind's `screenshot target=screen` raises a card every time;
  - `target=mind_view` does not.
