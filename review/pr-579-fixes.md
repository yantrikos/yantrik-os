| Finding | What changed | Test that would have caught it |
|---|---|---|
| BLOCKER 1: pill drawn behind app windows | The pill is its own window, `OsdWindow` (`ui/osd_window.slint`, title `Yantrik OSD`, frameless, transparent). labwc keeps it on top with a `windowRule` in `rc.xml` (`ToggleAlwaysOnTop`, no decoration, not in the taskbar or Alt+Tab) — the pattern the shell's own `ToggleOmnipresent` rule uses; no layer-shell (it is not in this repo, #564 is not on `main`). `App` no longer draws a pill. The window list and "which window is in front" ignore the OSD title. | `wire::osd::tests::the_pill_is_its_own_window_kept_above_app_windows_and_never_in_the_shell`, `showing_goes_through_one_present_that_gives_the_keyboard_back`, `control_levels_actions::tests::every_action_that_shows_the_pill_asks_hold_windows_first` |
